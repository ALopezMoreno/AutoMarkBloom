\noindent The model now grows a bud and opens it: petals grow over several
stages under their own stochastic growth automaton, push the sepals outward
and fold across layers (\cref{sec:packing}), and the opening stage then splays,
widens and uncurls them over 24 frames (\cref{sec:flowering}). Colour is next.
