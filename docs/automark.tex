\begin{abstract}
AutoMarkBloom grows a flower from a single integer seed in two stages.
A stochastic cellular automaton running on nested egg-shaped shells decides how
many petals a bud has and where each one sits. Each petal then becomes a thick
spline sheet, and the sheets are packed into the bud by minimising one smooth,
differentiable energy. A per-flower Markov chain will then open the flower, and
a logistic-map stage will colour it. This document explains each step as it is
implemented, states which parts are built, being reworked or still only
designed, and is updated whenever a stage changes.
\end{abstract}
